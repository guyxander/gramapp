01

Privacy, in plain language

Nadbooks Ventures, Ikotun, Lagos, Nigeria operates GramApp and is responsible for the personal data processed through the service. We use data to provide learning, tutor, security, and—when enabled—billing features. We do not sell your data.

02

Data we process

GramApp processes the information required to operate the service, including:

  • Account data used to create and secure your profile.
  • Learning progress such as lesson activity and grammar mastery.
  • Tutor interactions including assistance requests and messages.
  • Subscription status when paid access is enabled.
  • Optional voice recordings submitted for speaking feedback.

How long data is kept
