# Privacy notice

Nadbooks Ventures, Ikotun, Lagos, Nigeria, is the operator and data controller for Grammar Discovery. Privacy questions and requests may be sent to hello@hazi.ng.

Grammar Discovery stores account identity, lesson progress, tutor interactions, subscription status, moderation signals, and optional speaking recordings needed to provide the service. Google and Supabase process authentication; Supabase hosts application data; OpenRouter processes learner text submitted for AI feedback; Paystack processes billing when payments are enabled.

The app does not sell personal data. Contact details shared in tutoring conversations may be automatically flagged to protect users and keep assistance on-platform. Data is kept only for as long as needed to provide the service, meet security and accounting requirements, resolve disputes, or comply with law, after which it is deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

Users may request access, correction, export, or deletion of their personal data by emailing hello@hazi.ng. Nadbooks Ventures will process requests subject to identity verification and any retention required by applicable Nigerian law.
