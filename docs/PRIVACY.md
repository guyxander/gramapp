# Privacy notice

Grammar Discovery stores account identity, lesson progress, tutor interactions, subscription status, moderation signals, and optional speaking recordings needed to provide the service. Google and Supabase process authentication; Supabase hosts application data; OpenRouter processes learner text submitted for AI feedback; Paystack processes payments.

The app does not sell personal data. Contact details shared in tutoring conversations may be automatically flagged to protect users and keep assistance on-platform. Users may request access, correction, export, or deletion through the support contact published on the official site. Production launch requires replacing that contact and adding the controller identity, retention periods, hosting regions, and legally required country-specific disclosures.
